🧭 Decision Guide: DI vs CU

When should you use Document Intelligence vs Content Understanding? Use this reference guide to find the right service for your scenario.

🔵 Choose Document Intelligence if:

  • High-volume structured forms (invoices, receipts, IDs)
  • Need deterministic, cost-optimized processing
  • Already deployed DI in production
  • Simple text/layout extraction is sufficient

🟣 Choose Content Understanding if:

  • Extracting meaning from varied/unstructured documents
  • Need GenAI field inference without training
  • Working with audio, video, or images
  • Starting a new project (Microsoft's recommendation)
