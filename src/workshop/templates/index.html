💡 Key Insight

Microsoft recommends starting with Content Understanding for most new document processing scenarios. CU builds on DI's capabilities with GenAI-powered field inference, multimodal support, and improved accuracy. DI remains excellent for existing deployments and deterministic, high-throughput structured form extraction.
